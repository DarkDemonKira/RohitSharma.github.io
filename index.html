About

A little about how I work

I am Rohit, born and raised in Amlai, a town in Shahdol, Madhya Pradesh. School was fairly straightforward for me: I finished 10th from O.P.M. Higher Secondary School in 2019 stayed at the school for 12th in Commerce back, in 2021 and I am currently wrapping up my B.Com from Pandit Sambhunath Shukla University – the result is still awaited so fingers crossed.

While college was completed, I started working full‑time at AJIO in 2025 as a Customer Service Associate. I handled calls sorted out customer issues, the usual. What I did not expect was how quickly things moved: within six months I was promoted to a Process Supervisor role, where I now audit the kind of calls I used to handle and guide a small team of L1 associates through their day‑to‑day tasks.

Outside of targets and quality scores I am someone who likes things to run smoothly – process, calm conversations and helping new joiners feel a little less lost in their first week. I am still figuring a lot out like everyone but I am enjoying the climb so far.
